The name: Ay and Kora

AY+KORA

AyThe founder's initials. The studio carries his name.

KoraThe core. Every project starts below ground.

It began with a shell.

Two shells, traced and set on one ground line.

Sydney Opera House, Jørn Utzon. Photo: DXR, CC BY-SA 4.0

Reading the mark

  1. Ground

    The site line. Every building starts here.

  2. Foundation

    Two strokes go down into the earth. This is Kora, the core.

  3. Shells

    Two arches rise above the ground, like the Opera House shells.

  4. The A

    Follow the outer arch down into its column. That one stroke is the A of Aykora.

  5. The S

    The inner arch, the ground and the lower curve draw the S of Studio.

  6. The whole mark

    A and S on one ground. Rooted below, rising above.

Five file types, each with a job

Every version comes in all five, each file clearly named in the folder.

  • AIThe master file. Designers and printers edit from this.
  • SVGFor the website. Stays sharp at any size.
  • PDFFor printers and sign makers. Opens on any computer.
  • PNGNo background. For posts, slides and documents.
  • JPGWith a background. For WhatsApp and email.

RGB for screens, CMYK for printers

Screens make colour from light and printers make it from ink. The same navy needs different numbers in each, so every file comes in both. The colour then stays true on a phone and on paper.

RGB

Red, green and blue light. For phones, laptops, TVs and LED screens.

CMYK

Cyan, magenta, yellow and black ink. For cards, letterheads, boards and banners.
